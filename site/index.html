Will NIFTY close up or down today?

Every trading morning, before the market opens, our model reads what happened overnight in the US, in oil, in the rupee and in the Asian markets. Then it gives one answer: will NIFTY 50 close above or below yesterday's close.

01 How it works

India sleeps while the US trades. By the time NSE opens at 9:15 AM, a lot of news is already known. The model uses only information that is available before 9:00 AM.

  1. 2:30 AMUS market closesS&P 500, US VIX, crude oil and USD/INR moves are known.
  2. 5:30 AMTokyo opensThe opening gap of the Nikkei 225.
  3. 7:00 AMHong Kong opensThe opening gap of the Hang Seng.
  4. 7:50 AMModel runsIt is trained again on every finished day, then predicts today.
  5. 9:15 AMNSE opensThe prediction is already locked.
  6. 3:30 PMNSE closesThe real result is written next to the prediction.

All times are Indian Standard Time. The US close is 1:30 AM in the US summer.

Data

10 markets, since 2015

Daily prices of NIFTY 50, Bank NIFTY, India VIX, S&P 500, NASDAQ, US VIX, crude oil, USD/INR, Nikkei 225 and Hang Seng from Yahoo Finance. That gives usable days.

Features

inputs per day

18 describe yesterday's NIFTY (returns, RSI, MACD, volatility, volume). 6 describe the night: the US, oil, the rupee, and the opening gaps in Japan and Hong Kong.

Model

Three models vote

Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost each give a probability. The final answer is their average (soft voting). It was chosen on validation data, before the test set was touched.

03 Reality check: can you make money with it?

No. The model predicts yesterday's close to today's close. But most of that move happens at the open, as a gap, before anyone can buy. After the open, the direction is a coin flip (% correct).
